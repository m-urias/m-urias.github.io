# Macroeconomics

Welcome to the **Macroeconomics course site**.  This course is an intermediate-level course on macroeconomics.  We discuss the measurement of national output, inflation, and interest rates as well as develop economic theories to explain the relationship between these variables. In the first part of the course we develop a long-run perspective on the economy emphasizing capital and technology as the source of sustainable growth in GDP. We then build a model of the labor market where unemployment is an equilibrium phenomena arising from a dynamic process of job creation and job destruction. In the second part of the course pay attention to inflation and interest rates and discuss how the central bank can adjust these variables to influence real economy. In the final part of the course we develop a short-run perspective where the central bank has policy objectives that react to the state of the macroeconomy, which is subject to random shocks. This model captures key dynamics between central bank interest rate adjustments, inflation dynamics, and business cycle fluctuations in real GDP.
